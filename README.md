A 3D printed clock case for an aliexpress pcb clock my grandfather bought and soldered for himself.

My grandpa was always a maker, but recently he tried to get more into electronics. He bought himslef a clock soldering kit from aliexpress, and asked me to print him a case, so here are the files!
